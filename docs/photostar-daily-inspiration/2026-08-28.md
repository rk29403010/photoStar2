## Best next idea — Memory Bundles

Treat an event/person/place as a mixed-media evidence bundle rather than a collection of photos alone.

Example: `Skegness holiday, ~1964` could contain photographs, the reverse of prints, a postcard, handwritten letter, railway ticket, brochure, receipt, audio recollection, home-movie clip and an external map/context link. PhotoStar should keep each source distinct but make the relationship obvious.

This is different from Context Engine: Context Engine brings in external historical material; Memory Bundles connect the family's own surviving artefacts.

### Why now

Current family-archive products increasingly accept mixed family material rather than only photos. IZMe accepts documents, photos, letters and text; Andelin explicitly organises people, places, events and inherited objects; Memrial combines photos, home videos and stories; LifeArchive focuses on searchable journals and letters. These products validate the need, but PhotoStar can go further by preserving provenance and using image analysis to connect evidence automatically rather than merely filing everything into a collection.

Sources:
- https://izme.ai/
- https://andelin.app/
- https://memrial.com/
- https://lifearchive.io/

## Practical UI — Evidence Tray

Open a photo and show a restrained `Related evidence` tray:

- another photo from the same probable event
- reverse-side scan / handwriting
- letter mentioning the same people/place/date
- ticket/programme/receipt likely from the event
- audio recollection linked to the photo

Each suggestion says why it is related and remains a proposal until accepted. This reuses the existing Claims/Evidence and Explainable Discovery ideas rather than creating opaque automatic links.

Effort: low-medium for manual links and proposal UI; medium for automatic suggestions.

## Technical experiment — visual document retrieval

ColPali/ColVision is particularly relevant for scanned family documents because it retrieves document pages from their visual appearance and text/layout without requiring a brittle OCR-first pipeline. The official `illuin-tech/colpali` repository includes ColPali, ColQwen2 and newer ColVision variants; release v0.3.16 was published in May 2026.

Official code: https://github.com/illuin-tech/colpali
Official models/project: https://huggingface.co/vidore
Paper/project: https://huggingface.co/papers/2407.01449

No author-hosted general upload-and-search demo was found. The code and checkpoints are usable locally, but this should be benchmarked against PhotoStar's actual scanned letters, tickets, album captions and mixed documents before integration.

A sensible first benchmark is not generic RAG. Test whether queries such as `letter mentioning Skegness`, `document with this surname`, `ticket from Nottingham` or a reference crop can retrieve the right page from a few hundred scans.

## Product direction — one event, many forms of evidence

Add a first-class `MemoryBundle` or reuse/extend an event/group entity with typed relationships rather than forcing every item into an album.

Possible relationships:

- `depictsEvent`
- `createdDuringEvent`
- `mentionsEvent`
- `evidenceForDate`
- `evidenceForPlace`
- `recollectionAbout`
- `derivedFrom`

The important distinction is that a railway ticket may be evidence for a journey without depicting it, and a 2026 audio interview may discuss a 1964 holiday without being an artefact from 1964.

Effort: medium, mainly data-model work.

## Creative concept — The Table

Instead of a timeline or gallery, present one family episode as if its surviving material had been laid out on a table: photographs, letter, ticket, map, handwriting, audio and later notes. Selecting an item subtly shows its connections to the others. No generated collage is required; the interface itself becomes a digital equivalent of opening a family box on a kitchen table.

The 2026 `Affective Archives` exhibition is a useful artistic reference because it deliberately combines photographs, notes and ephemera to treat family memory as something assembled from heterogeneous surviving material rather than a sequence of isolated images: https://greenspacemiami.org/exhibition/affective-archives-2026-wopha-exhibition/

Effort: medium once Memory Bundles exist; creative value high.

## Best next experiment

Take one well-understood family event with perhaps 20-50 related items. Manually create a bundle containing photographs plus any reverse scans, letters, tickets, maps or audio that survive. Prototype only three interactions:

1. open the event and see all evidence types together;
2. click any item and see why the other items are linked;
3. add/remove a proposed relationship without changing the underlying files.

If that feels materially richer than an album, then add automatic retrieval. The core idea is simple: PhotoStar should preserve not just photographs of a family event, but the surviving evidence around it.
