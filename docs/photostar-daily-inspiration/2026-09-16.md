## Best next idea — Temporal Triage / Undated Inbox

Turn the new Undated count from a passive statistic into a prioritized work queue. Do not ask the user to date photos one-by-one in arbitrary order. Rank undated photographs by how much of the surrounding archive they can unlock.

Candidate priority signals:

- direct date text/stamp/back-of-print evidence;
- known people with birth/death/age constraints;
- membership in a roll, album, event, duplicate or photograph family;
- dated neighbours in scan/film order;
- clothing/object/background clues;
- links to already dated representations of the same Photograph;
- number of other undated items whose range would tighten if this one were resolved.

A review card should show an interval and evidence, e.g. `1964–1967`, not manufacture an exact date. One confirmation can then tighten related items conservatively.

## Research/product synthesis

Mylio LifeCalendar already supports fuzzy dates/ranges and undated scans. MyHeritage PhotoDater estimates dates and explicitly stores accepted estimates as estimated GEDCOM dates rather than exact facts. Timeline Scan combines front/back text, people/ages, visual clues, filenames and neighbouring scans, and describes strongly evidenced photos as anchors that help place nearby scans. PhotoStar should synthesize these ideas with its own evidence/proposition model: preserve each clue separately, calculate constraints across related photographs, and prioritize the human decisions with the highest expected archive-wide payoff.

The CHR 2023 paper `Blind Dates: Examining the Expression of Temporality in Historical Photographs` found zero-shot OpenCLIP weak for historical dating, while fine-tuning improved performance and certain visible categories such as people and vehicles carried temporal signal. I could not find a clearly author-maintained runnable repository or hosted demo for this specific study; use it as research evidence, not an implementation dependency.

## Practical implementation

First version needs no new model:

1. derive conservative date bounds from existing exact/fuzzy dates, GEDCOM life dates, same-Photograph representations and ordered groups;
2. propagate only logically valid bounds;
3. score undated items by `solvability × downstream impact`;
4. expose the top 20 in a review queue;
5. record confirmations as human decisions/evidence rather than silently overwriting machine estimates.

Later, add model-derived visual-date observations as another evidence source, never as automatic truth.

## Creative concept — Time Fog

Let uncertain photos visibly inhabit time without pretending precision. A photo believed to be from 1962–1968 appears as a soft six-year span in the timeline; a securely dated wedding is a sharp point. Related photos can form overlapping clouds that collapse toward dates as captions, people, rolls and events are resolved. This makes uncertainty legible rather than hiding undated material in a bucket.

## Best next experiment

Use 200 genuinely undated photos from several known rolls/albums. Hide any known ground truth. Compare arbitrary manual review against Temporal Triage. Measure: number of photos placed into a useful year/range per 20 human decisions; downstream ranges tightened per confirmation; false precision; and time spent. If prioritization doubles the amount of archive chronology recovered from the same 20 decisions, build the Undated Inbox before adding another dating model.

Core principle: **do not ask the user to solve every undated photograph; ask the few date questions that make the rest easier.**
