# PhotoStar2 Daily Inspiration — 26 October 2026
## Album Archaeology — Preserve the Page, Discover the Photographs

**Best next idea:** treat a scanned physical album page as a first-class archival object, with independently navigable photo regions and annotations. Unlike previous Photo Echo / Object Trail / Moment Picker ideas, this preserves the original *arrangement* and handwritten context of a physical album rather than matching individual pictures or choosing burst frames.

### Proposed feature
**Album Archaeology** opens a full-page scan in a zoomable viewer; a user draws or adjusts boxes around each photograph, a caption, or an empty mounting space. Clicking a region opens its photograph without losing the parent page. A breadcrumb returns to `Album → Page 12 → Photograph 3`.

Preserve three distinct entities:
1. **Original page scan:** immutable master with checksum and scan provenance.
2. **Page region:** polygon/box, order, type (`photograph`, `handwriting`, `clipping`, `empty_mount`), human status, source page.
3. **Optional derived photo asset:** virtual crop / rectification transform; no duplicate image bytes required until export.

Physical order is evidence of *album arrangement*, not necessarily capture chronology. A caption beside two photographs should remain unassigned until confirmed. Keep human edits separately from generated proposals, with reversible reject/accept decisions.

### Current software and synthesis
- [Photomyne official help](https://photomyne.com/faq/What-can-I-do-with-the-Photomyne-app%3F): captures full album pages, auto-crops multiple photographs, supports captions/audio. Useful scanning UX, but PhotoStar should emphasise durable parent-page provenance.
- [ScanSpeeder](https://scanspeeder.com/): Windows flatbed workflow with album-page scanning and auto-cropping; useful comparator for fast detection and rotation.
- [Tropy selections](https://docs.tropy.org/in-the-item-view/selections): region-specific notes/metadata and export; useful evidence model, not a ready-made PhotoStar gallery.
- [FamilySearch Memories](https://www.familysearch.org/en/help/helpcenter/article/how-do-i-use-familysearch-memories-to-preserve-my-ancestors-life-stories): people, albums, documents, audio and stories; PhotoStar could preserve page geometry while linking photos to identities.
- [Library of Congress scrapbook guidance](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/care/scrapbk.html) stresses that album arrangement and handwritten material can be intrinsic historical evidence. [US National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/preservation/holdings-maintenance/photographs) warns against detaching prints from album pages. This feature is as much preservation as convenience.

### Model / technique lab (official links, verified availability of published resources; not tested on album scans)
1. **OpenCV contours + four-point perspective transform**: conventional baseline for high-contrast rectangular prints. Official [source](https://github.com/opencv/opencv) and [tutorials](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/d0c/group__imgproc__shape.html). Runnable local code/docs; **no dedicated official hosted album-page detector demo found**. Start here.
2. **DocLayout-YOLO (2024)**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12628), [authors' official GitHub](https://github.com/opendatalab/DocLayout-YOLO), [project-team Hugging Face demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/opendatalab/DocLayout-YOLO) (listed Running on Zero at research time). Document-layout detector, **not trained specifically to identify pasted family photographs**. AGPL-3.0 code; check licensing and domain shift. Benchmark only.
3. **SAM 2 / 2.1 (Meta FAIR)**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00714), [official Meta code and image notebooks](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2), [official Meta demo](https://sam2.metademolab.com/demo). Interactive masks may help overlapping or irregular print borders. Code/checkpoints and local demo are provided; official online demo exists but was **not successfully exercised here**. Official repository recommends WSL on Windows; heavyweight relative to v1. No need to adopt for rectangles.

### Art, historical context and experimental concept
The [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/care/scrapbk.html) notes that scrapbook pages can combine photographs, newspaper clippings, handwritten notes and three-dimensional keepsakes. [Jo Spence's *Beyond the Family Album*](https://www.macba.cat/en/obra/r2825-beyond-the-family-album/) challenges what conventional family albums show or omit.

**Experimental: The Missing Photograph.** Detect a rectangular empty mounting space and treat it as an *absence*, not a blank to fill with AI. Show its neighbouring photos and any handwritten caption; optionally search loose scans for a possible match. Only a human-confirmed match fills the slot. A story view could explore what is documented, missing or remembered about a particular album page. This connects album composition to the history of how the family chose to present itself, without fabricating images.

### Best next experiment
Use **20 album-page scans**, containing about **80 prints**, plus pages with overlapping photographs, faded borders, annotations and empty corners. Time manual region markup. Compare basic OpenCV contours against DocLayout-YOLO and optional SAM 2 for difficult pages. Metrics: photo-region recall, false crops, caption-to-photo misassociation, human correction time, and retention of page order/provenance. A useful first target is **under 30 seconds of manual correction per ordinary 4-photo page**; treat this as a target, not an observed result.

**Recommendation:** implement the read-only album-page viewer and reversible manual regions first. It uses PhotoStar's existing `assets`, `album_items`, image-region concepts and related-photo architecture without adding AI dependencies or a second archive. If users find page navigation valuable, add automated layout suggestions.
