## Proposal: Lost Roll Workbench
Scattered scanned negatives and prints often preserve fragments of their original sequence through filenames, scan folders, adjacent frames, repeated people, lighting, clothing and scenery. Show candidate strips in a virtual contact sheet with explicit **known order**, **suggested neighbour** and **unknown** states. Let users drag frames to reorder, split rolls, mark missing frames, and preserve human decisions separately from rebuildable suggestions. Never infer capture chronology merely from scan order.

Start with existing folder/number and related-photo evidence; add a candidate-pair score and explainable provenance. A small manual prototype is about 4–7 days; automatic sequence ranking another 5–10 days plus testing. Provide virtualized strip thumbnails for large collections and jump-to-frame from ordinary gallery. Avoid destructive filename renaming.

## Product research
- [OpenEnlarge](https://openenlarge.io/) uses roll-wide contact sheets and shared processing; its official site advertises open-source code and Windows support.
- [Lento Dock Connect](https://www.lento.fun/dock-connect/) presents physical roll order in a scan table.
- [Tropy](https://www.tropy.org/) groups multiple images into archival items with notes.
- [PhotoStructure](https://photostructure.com/) distinguishes duplicates, edits and copies; sequence identity must remain separate from variant identity.
- [FolioSort](https://www.foliosort.app/) advertises local date/event grouping and reversible file operations.
- [Rollfilm](https://rollfilm.org/) combines import, contact sheets, editing and optional Immich sync.

## Research and code
- [LightGlue (ICCV 2023)](https://github.com/cvg/LightGlue) matches local image features, useful for confirming overlapping scene content or alternative scans, **not** a direct chronological-order predictor. Official authors' code includes a Colab and linked Hugging Face demo; benchmark before adopting.
- [imagecluster](https://github.com/elcorto/imagecluster) offers image-content clustering with optional timestamp weighting. Author-maintained source and examples exist; no verified author-hosted interactive upload demo.
- [OpenCV](https://github.com/opencv/opencv) supplies local feature matching and homography tools; official tutorials at https://docs.opencv.org/ . This is the lightweight baseline before ML.
Test on 20 deliberately shuffled scan groups with mixed scan orders, duplicates, missing frames and overlapping scenes. Measure adjacent-frame precision, incorrect forced links and manual review time.

## Creative concept: The Missing Frames
Render a reconstructed contact sheet with deliberate empty slots for missing frames. A family member can attach a recollection to a gap without generating fictional photographs. The gaps themselves communicate what the archive cannot tell us, while the recovered sequence tells a story of how a day unfolded.

**Best next experiment:** Build a read-only candidate contact-sheet prototype from existing folder, filename-number and related-photo features. Compare against manually reconstructed strips. Fix or isolate the filename-sort gallery regression independently.
