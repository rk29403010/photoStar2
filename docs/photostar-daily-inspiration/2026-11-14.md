## Product
A Scan Pairing Inbox suggests front/back associations using scan batch, filenames, dimensions and image characteristics. Human review confirms, rejects or defers links. One envelope may contain several prints, and different physical copies of the same photograph may have different handwritten notes. Keep physical-object relationships separate from visual variants and machine claims.

## Effort
Read-only pairing/contact sheet: 2–4 developer days; reversible artifact-side relationships: 4–7 days; inscription-region transcription and correction: 4–8 days; optional local OCR benchmark: 3–6 days. Never automatically turn OCR text into verified names or dates.
