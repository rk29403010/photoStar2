# PhotoStar2 inspiration — 2026-11-17

## Best next idea: Representative Set / Contact Sheet Curator

Build a reversible, read-only collection curation view that proposes a **representative set** of images from an album, capture sequence or similarity group, without deleting or hiding unique archival evidence. The purpose is to make large sets approachable for research, sharing and storytelling.

### Design
1. Open an album or selected set; choose “Make a contact sheet”. Show 1–3 representatives per cluster, with a collapsed “more like these” count and keyboard-driven expand.
2. Choose representatives with explicit reasons: sharpness, unobscured faces, readable inscriptions, fuller frame, distinct subjects or moments, historical evidence; do not simply pick biggest file.
3. Preserve distinct prints, annotations, and restoration variants as linked alternatives. Reject proposed representatives without changing human identity/evidence decisions.
4. Export a printable contact sheet or research shortlist with captions, confidence and source pointers. Offer density controls and a pinned “not yet reviewed” lane.
5. Never delete or rewrite originals. A selection is a user-reversible presentation preference.

### Sources and comparisons
- Immich duplicates utility: https://docs.immich.app/features/duplicates-utility/ — user review, keep multiple, stack; preselects by bytes and EXIF richness. A warning that size is not archival value.
- PhotoPrism duplicate documentation: https://docs.photoprism.app/user-guide/library/duplicates/ — exact checksums vs related-file stacks.
- LibrePhotos: https://docs.librephotos.com/ — review-centric face workflow and collection browsing.
- Tropy: https://www.tropy.org/ — archival object context and notes.
- Gramps Web: https://www.grampsweb.org/features/ — family relationship context.
- darktable: https://www.darktable.org/ — non-destructive edit variants.
- pyIQA official authors' implementation: https://github.com/chaofengc/IQA-PyTorch — runnable local image-quality models (MUSIQ, TOPIQ, NIMA, etc); no verified official hosted interactive upload demo. Assess for historical images separately; aesthetic score is not documentary value.
- NIMA implementation by idealo: https://github.com/idealo/image-quality-assessment — runnable research implementation, no verified author-hosted demo. Models trained on modern quality/aesthetic datasets can unfairly penalise old scans.

### Creative extension: The Contact Sheet as a story
Generate an interactive “one day in 12 frames” strip from a family occasion, preserving sequence and revealing alternates beneath each frame. Contrast what was documented with what remains absent; avoid AI-invented historical moments. A small printable sheet becomes a family research prompt, with QR/link back to the full evidence.

### Proposed experiment
On 30 groups spanning burst shots, rescans, annotated backs, deteriorated prints and restored derivatives, manually label essential unique evidence. Compare naive largest-file selection against diversity + archival-evidence rules. Measure essential-evidence recall (target 100% in expanded view), false suppression, review time, keyboard navigation speed and 10k-photo grid responsiveness.

**Recommendation:** Ship a read-only representative contact-sheet view using existing album/sequence/related-photo signals before adding a neural quality scorer. Rough effort 4–8 developer days for a basic UI and export prototype, with more time for benchmarking and provenance integration. No application code changed.
